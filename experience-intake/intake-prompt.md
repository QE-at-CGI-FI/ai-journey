# Ask to send colleagues

Copy-paste and send as-is, or adapt.

---

I'm mapping out how people around here are actually using AI, and I'd love
yours. Think of it like a school essay, except the assignment is to brag:
what have you managed to do with AI that you're a little proud of? Any use of 
*yours* is brag-worthy, so dig deep. 

No template, no checklist to fill in — just write it as freeform text, your
own way. Could be one paragraph or several. Cover whatever's true for you:
day-to-day stuff (autocomplete, asking it questions, summarizing things),
things you've built, habits you've changed how you work around, things
you've tried that didn't quite work, things you've seen elsewhere and want
to get to. Concrete beats vague — name the tool, the project, the client,
the thing you actually did, rather than "I use AI a lot for testing."

Using AI to help you write it is completely fine — draft it with Claude,
ChatGPT, whatever, if that's easier than staring at a blank page.

Send it back to me whenever it's ready.
